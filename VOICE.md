# Voice

Feld sounds like a principal who has sat on the floor and is writing it down that night. Literate, concrete, unhurried. Never a campaign.

## Stance

- Talk to an operator. Assume they have run the process they are reading about, or are responsible for the person who has.
- Name the work. “Tuesday’s exception queue” is a Feld sentence. “Optimize your workflow” is not.
- Credit the conjecture. Levie’s sentences stay in quotation, with his name. Feld’s sentences are what the firm will do about them.
- Stay after the interesting part. The site’s affection is for maintenance: decay dates, night coverage, the second model, the person who remains when the pod leaves the building.
- Be willing to refuse. Several pages say what the firm will not install. That is voice, not legal boilerplate.

## Sentence

Mix a long sentence that earns its clauses with a short one after it. The short one is the nail. Paragraphs are short enough to read on a phone held in a corridor.

Use the second person sparingly, and only when the page is a letter (pricing, the three envelopes, the begin form). Everywhere else, “the company,” “the pod,” “the keeper.”

Numbers are instruments. When a field record uses a figure, the page labels it as part of a composite. Never invent a percentage and let it look measured.

## Words the site uses

Install, remain, release, keeper, pod, floor, queue, binding, passage, redraw, loop, eval, decay, weather, handback, shadow week, premises, doctrine, composite, attestation, exception, permit, reserve, close, intake.

“Weather” means model change: a new release, a new capability, a quiet regression on a Thursday. It is not a metaphor for vibes. If the sentence works with “model change” substituted, the usage is legal.

## Words the site does not use

Leverage, unlock, seamless, cutting-edge, empower, revolutionize, synergy, next-gen, disrupt, solution (as a verb or as a foggy noun), holistic, robust (except about a test), delve, tapestry, landscape (as a metaphor), journey, supercharge, elevate, harness (as a verb of enthusiasm), AI-powered, intelligent automation, digital transformation, co-pilot (as a product pose), magic, simply, just (as a minimizer).

No exclamation marks. No emoji. No “we’re excited.” No rhetorical questions in headlines.

## The contrast problem

The conjecture is built on a difference between installing software and installing work. The site may stage that difference once, on `/enablement` and inside the quoted post. Other pages should say what Feld does. A headline shaped like “Not a vendor. A partner.” is off-voice. A headline shaped like “Remain for the weather.” is on-voice.

## Titles

- Display headlines are sentences or noun-phrases with a period when they are sentences. “Install the work.” carries the period. “Passage” does not.
- Title case is for the mast wordmark only. Headlines are sentence case.
- Page titles in the browser: `Passage — Feld Company`. Home is `Feld Company — The deployment layer`.

## Field records and people

Open every field record with the visible kicker: “Composite. The shape of the work, not a client.”

People pages may read as a real roster. The project’s readme and the people packet’s editor note record that the cast is invented. Do not add portraits that resemble real individuals. If a portrait is generated, it is a drawing, not a photograph of a face: a tool, a desk, a hand, a city at shift change.

## UI microcopy

The interface is the same firm, speaking more quietly.

| Moment | Copy |
|---|---|
| Primary button, idle | The verb of the page: “Begin a deployment”, “Send the letter”, “See the week” |
| Primary, loading | “Sending” or “Working”. Never “Please wait!!!” |
| Primary, success | Stay on the page. A slip confirms. |
| Disabled | Still say the verb. The reason sits in helper text. |
| Text field label | Noun, not instruction: “Process”, “System of record” |
| Helper | One sentence of consequence |
| Error, email | “That address cannot receive a reply.” |
| Error, required | “The letter needs this.” |
| Error, summary | “Four answers are missing. They are marked on the form.” |
| Checkbox | A full sentence the person can agree to |
| Select empty | “Choose one” |
| Ledger empty | “Nothing in this queue. When work arrives it will sit here with an owner.” |
| Ledger loading | “Opening the queue.” Skeleton rows, no spinner-as-personality |
| Ledger no matches | “No rows match this filter. The queue itself is unchanged.” |
| Slip, success | “The letter is with us. A person reads it.” |
| Slip, error | “The letter did not leave this browser.” |
| Slip, warning | “This draft has a decay date of Friday.” |
| Slip, info | “Night survey is on. The tokens have changed. The words have not.” |
| Chamber title, confirm | “Send this letter?” |
| Chamber, danger confirm | “Withdraw the draft?” |
| Alert | A statement, plus one action: “Close” |
| Crumb root | “Feld” |
| Mobile menu button | “Index”, not a bare hamburger with no name |
| Theme toggle | “Day ledger” / “Night survey” |
| 404, if built | “This page was never in the quire.” Link home. |
| Footer colophon | See `CHROME.md` |

Validation appears after blur or submit, not on first keystroke. The error is tied to the field with text, not color alone.

## Humor

Dry, and at most once a page. The unlisted-work page and the mid-market page earn a faint smile. The civic page and the health page do not joke.

## A sample, to tune against

On voice:

> The agent can draft the reserve note. A handler releases it. The eval is twelve real files from March, owned by the person who used to check them by hand, and it decays on the first Monday of each month unless someone says it is still true.

Off voice:

> Our cutting-edge AI solution seamlessly empowers insurers to unlock next-gen claims efficiency.

If a sentence could sit on any AI company’s homepage, cut it.
