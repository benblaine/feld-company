### A log

Entries are newest first. This log is a composite, written to show the form.

**12 March · Refused.** A faster model arrived for the draft step. Two ugly cases drifted, both toward a coverage the prior ruling had closed. The queue stayed on the previous model. Ruth wrote the refusal. The sponsor was disappointed for an afternoon and relieved by Friday, when a peer's floor did not refuse.

**2 March · Retested and kept.** A small capability: better reading of photo metadata. The ordinary cases improved. The refusal cases still refused. We switched the metadata path only. The prose path stayed. Partial switches are allowed. Vague switches are not.

**18 February · Held.** The vendor's note was kind and the change was large. We held for one decay cycle. Holding is a decision, so it gets a line, not silence.

**4 February · Retired a case.** "Neighbor-check, windshield" had become ordinary because the binding learned the prior-ruling field. We retired it from the ugly set and wrote a new ugly case from last week's file. A set that never retires is a museum.

### How a year of weather is sold

It is part of the engagement, not a retainer for vague advice. A visit or a written decision at each material change, a standing hour with the keeper, and a right for the customer to call when their own vendor surprises them. The pricing letter says this without a rate card.

### What weather is not

A standing committee. A slide that says "continuous improvement." An automatic upgrade because the contract allowed it. Automation of the upgrade is the opposite of this labor.

### Close

If the current vendor upgrades you without a log, you do not yet have weather. You have weather happening to you.
