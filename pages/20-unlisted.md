**Dek:** He left the sentence open on purpose. These are the items that stall an install after the technical labors look done. They are not soft. They are the rest of the system.

### The deputy

Every named person has a life. The loop card's deputy is not a courtesy. On the week the release-holder is away, the deputy is the control. Write their name before you need it. If they decline the duty, believe them, and find someone who accepts.

### The freeze

Quarter-end, open enrollment, storm season, a regulator's visit, a plant shutdown. The freeze is announced late and runs long. A pod that schedules shadow week across a freeze has chosen theatre. Ask for the freeze calendar in the first ten days, and assume it is incomplete.

### The exception-keeper

There is a person who knows why the strange files are strange. They are often not the sponsor, and often not senior. Cartography fails politely when it never sits with them. Pay the sit with time, not with a workshop where they are one of twenty.

### The bonus

Someone is paid for speed. Someone else is paid for never being the name on a bad release. An agent that changes the speed without changing the blame will be used exactly as far as the blame allows, and no further. Ask how the floor is paid. Put the answer on the map.

### The regulator, the auditor, the harmed person

Someone who is not in the kickoff will read the output later. Write the attestation as if they are your reader. If you cannot explain a draft's path in a page, the path is too clever.

### The vendor who will not grant the scope

A binding sometimes depends on a signature the incumbent vendor is in no hurry to give. This is not a surprise to be discovered in month four. It is a question for week one, and sometimes a reason to refuse the process.

### The favor

Some queues exist because a team is kind, not because a policy named them. Automating a favor can destroy it. If the work is a favor, say so in the redraw, and decide whether the favor should become a duty before you give it to an agent that does not know how to be kind.

### Close

Add your own line when you see it. The unlisted page in a real engagement is a living document. This one is only the seed.
