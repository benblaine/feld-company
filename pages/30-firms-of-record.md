## Copy

**Browser title:** Firms of record — Feld Company

**Eyebrow:** Industries · Firms of record

**Dek:** Firms of record sell a name on a letter. Anything we install has to make that name easier to defend, not easier to bypass. The work is inside the matter and the workpaper. The signature is a person.

### The matter

Research, chronology, a first draft of a memo in the firm's voice, a bundle of the documents actually in the file. An agent may draft these inside the wall the firm and its client have agreed. Privilege is an access problem: who may read, which tenant, what is never sent to a model that retains. Idris reviews that binding before any matter is in it. A pilot that "just tries a tool" on a live matter is a breach of this page.

### The workpaper

Audit workpapers have a reviewer because the standard says so, not because the firm is sentimental. An agent may draft a description of a procedure that was performed, from the evidence the binder holds. It may not describe a procedure that was not performed. The eval is full of that temptation. The reviewer signs. The agent does not.

### The signature block

We leave it alone, and we design up to its edge. A draft ends with a blank block and the name of the person whose duty it is. Autocomplete on a signature is a joke in bad taste. We will not build it, including "for convenience."

### Close

If you want the firm to feel modern, start with the bundle and the chronology. The signature was never the bottleneck. The night before it was.
