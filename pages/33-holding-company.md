## Copy

**Browser title:** Holding company — Feld Company

**Meta description:** Many businesses, one board question: what is our pattern? A doctrine the businesses can use, and the freedom for a business to say it does not fit.

**Eyebrow:** Scale · The holding company

**Headline:** One pattern. A right to say it does not fit.

**Dek:** A group of companies asks a reasonable question from the board: what is our pattern with agents? The wrong answer is a single platform mandate. The answer we will help write is a doctrine: the labors, the loop, the eval, the weather log, and a way for a business to decline the pattern without being treated as slow.

### The doctrine

A short document, in the group's language, covering the eight labors and the refusals. It is not a tool standard. Businesses run different systems. The doctrine says what must be true of an install, whoever does it: a release name, an access design, a living eval, a weather log, a keeper. Feld can be the firm that writes it and the firm that performs the first install. The doctrine should survive us.

### The first business

Choose the business where the process is real and the managing director will tolerate a negative result. A doctrine proved only on the friendly subsidiary is a speech. The first business is a test of the doctrine, and the doctrine is allowed to change afterward. Version it. The version is a form of weather.

### The decline

A business in the group may say: our process is not ready, our vendor will not grant scope, our regulator has a question, our people are mid-negotiation. The doctrine treats that as an outcome, recorded, not as a failure to adopt. Adoption targets are how groups lie to themselves. We will not staff a program whose success is measured that way.

### Close

Bring the board's actual question, in their words. We will answer it with a doctrine or we will tell you the group is not ready to have one.
