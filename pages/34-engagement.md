**Dek:** We sell an install and a year of weather. The statement of work names the process, the artifacts, and the conditions under which either side may stop. It does not name a transformation.

### What is named at the start

The process, in the company's words. The system of record and the system of use. The release name, or a date by which it will be named, early enough to matter. What must never be automated. The freeze calendar, as far as it is known. The keeper, or an honest gap where the keeper will be.

If several of these are blank, the engagement starts as cartography only, priced as cartography, with a decision at the end to continue or to part.

### The artifacts

A map. An access design. A catalog of bindings. A redraw on one page. A loop card. An eval set with a decay date. A weather log. An attestation the floor actually sees. A handback note the keeper has signed as operable.

Slides are not artifacts. A slide may describe an artifact. It does not replace one.

### Stopping

Either side may stop at the cartography decision, after shadow week, or at a weather refusal that changes the economics. Stopping is written into the engagement so that continuing is a choice. A pod that cannot be dismissed is not a partner. It is a new legacy system.

### Close

The first ninety days are the calendar version of this page. The pricing letter is the commercial version. Neither is a rate card.
