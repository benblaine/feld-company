## Copy

**Browser title:** Shadow week — Feld Company

**Meta description:** Five days of the agent drafting beside the human, before any release right. Disagreement is the point of the week.

**Eyebrow:** Practice · Shadow week

**Headline:** The week the draft meets the file.

**Dek:** Before anyone may release an agent's work, the draft runs beside the person who already does the job. Five days. The disagreements are the harvest. Agreement is noted and not celebrated.

**Kicker:** Composite notebook. A claims floor, because we have already asked you to picture one. The shape fits other floors.

### Monday

Twelve files. The draft is fluent and, on three of them, early. It writes a cause the handler has not accepted. She marks the three. We do not "fix the prompt" in the evening and call it learning. We tag the cases.

### Tuesday

The prior-ruling field was in the system and not in the binding. Samir adds it, read-only. The same three files are run again. Two calm down. One still invents a delay. That one becomes an ugly case with a name.

### Wednesday

The handler is faster than the draft on ordinary files and slower on the file with bad photos. This is useful. The job sentence gains a clause: if the photo metadata is missing, refuse and send it to the exception desk. The agent is not asked to try harder at seeing.

### Thursday

A sponsor watches for an hour and wants a release by Friday. The notebook is shown, including Tuesday's invention. The sponsor agrees to wait. This conversation is part of shadow week, and the person who can have it is part of the pod.

### Friday

The week closes with a written choice. Narrow release on ordinary side-glass files, starting next week, R. releasing. Everything else still shadowed. Or no release, if Friday's files had surprised us. In this composite, the narrow release is earned. On another floor it would not be. Both endings are written into the method so the week is not a ceremony for a decision already made.

### Close

If you want a pilot that cannot end in "not yet," you want a demonstration. We can recommend one. We do not staff it.
