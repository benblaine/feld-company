### The rule that held

No agent in the switching decision. Escalation at most, on a drill, never as a new instruction on a live desk. The pod's worth was measured by an absence. The shift lead noticed the absence and trusted the next morning more because of it.

### The morning

The draft log proposed four lines from alarms and calls the binding could see. It missed a phone call that happened off the recorded line. The shift lead added the call in their own words and corrected one timestamp by a minute. The correction is the attestation. A log without a visible correction would have been a smoother lie.

We added the missed-call pattern to the eval as a refusal case: if the binding did not see it, the draft must not imply that nothing else happened. The job sentence gained a last line. "What you did not see, say you did not see."

### Their weather and ours

Two weeks later a model upgrade looked helpful for prose. Their season was still unsettled. The log says Held, for their weather, not for ours. The install paused. Nobody on the desk had to argue for this. It was already the page they had read.

### Close

The last record is a civic queue, where the reader of the sentence is the person the file is about.
