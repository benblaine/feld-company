## Copy

**Browser title:** Field record, benefits intake — Feld Company

**Meta description:** A composite of a civic benefits queue: a case jacket, an adverse reason the applicant can follow, and an appeal line the eval treats as mandatory.

**Eyebrow:** Records · Composite

**Headline:** An adverse letter a person can follow.

**Kicker:** Composite. The shape of the work, not a client. Not a real agency. Not a real applicant.

**Dek:** Intake for a support that depends on a wage document and a rule about which month counts. The queue was slow in a way that hurt, and fast in a way that would have hurt differently. We were hired to draft the jacket and the reason, not to decide.

### The jacket

Three blocks, kept separate all the way into the letter: what the applicant said, what the document said, what the rule did. Early drafts blended them, because blended prose reads well. The caseworkers rejected the blend, correctly. Fluency was the defect. The job sentence now forbids blending.

### The appeal line

One draft omitted the appeal because the letter felt "cleaner." That case is in the eval as a refusal. It pauses the queue. The caseworker who caught it is the keeper. She was not senior. She was the person who knew the exception, which is the unlisted labor, paid in hours on her week.

### Language

The desk already served three languages. The letter ships in those three or it does not ship. We did not add a language, and we did not drop one to make the first month smaller. The install was slower. The alternative was a faster wrong.

### What we will say about time

The queue is still a queue. Some files are faster because the jacket arrives assembled. The adverse files are not faster at the expense of the appeal. We will not cite a percentage. The keeper's standard was: a person can tell why, and can tell what to do next, without calling. In the composite, the letters that meet that standard are the ones that leave.

### Close

This is the last record. The journal, after it, is the firm thinking in longer sentences.
