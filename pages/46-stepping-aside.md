## Copy

**Browser title:** Stepping aside — Feld Company

**Meta description:** An essay on leaving a floor after an install: not vanishing, not staying forever, and not calling a party a handback.

**Eyebrow:** Journal · Essay 01

**Headline:** Stepping aside is a season, not an exit.

**Dek:** Software implementation trained a generation of firms to leave. The work was a known category. The customer ran it. The conjecture is right that an agent install does not behave this way. The question is what "remain" looks like when remaining forever would also be a failure.

### The old completion

There was a kind of completion that made sense. A category of software, understood well enough that the implementation had edges, was placed in the building. Training happened. The vendor stepped back. The customer kept running, and the running was the point of the purchase. Calling that pattern thin is unfair to the people who did it well. Many of them were the most competent person on the floor for a year. The pattern fit the thing being installed.

### What is different about work output

An agent in a process keeps producing. The draft on a Thursday in November is the product, not the go-live in June. The model under the draft may have changed. The person's judgment may have changed. The unofficial workbook may have quietly become the control again. A firm that has stepped all the way back is not watching any of that. A firm that has not stepped aside at all has become a new dependency, billed monthly, resented reasonably.

So the motion is aside, not back. We are no longer in the chair. We can still be called to the table. The handback is the list of what must be true before that motion is honest: keeper, runbook, binding they can revoke, eval with a date, log that includes refusals, loop card with a deputy, attestation the floor can see, and a specific right to call.

### The two bad exits

Vanishing is the first. It looks like efficiency. The keeper discovers the first material weather alone, and the queue either drifts or is switched off in fear. Both are rational. The engagement failed them.

Lingering is the second. It looks like care. Every decay meeting has a Feld face in it, and the keeper never becomes the person who can pause the queue without asking. The company has rented a judgment. The pricing letter exists to make this expensive and visible, so it is chosen rather than drifted into. Even then, we would rather lose the renewal than keep a keeper ornamental.

### Month ten

The conversation about renewing or ending belongs in month ten. Ending needs a season, not a Friday. Renewing needs a reason: a second process, a year of weather they do not want to hold alone, a doctrine for the group. "Because the contract expires" is not a reason. It is a calendar.

### A word to the person on the floor

If you are the keeper reading this before you accept the name: you are allowed to ask for hours in your actual week. You are allowed to refuse the name. An unsigned handback note is a true statement, and a pod that respects it is a pod you can trust with the months before.

### Close

The next essay is about the firms that used to do the leaving, and whether they can learn this season.
