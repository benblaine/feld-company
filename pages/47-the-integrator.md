**Browser title:** The integrator's window — Feld Company

**Meta description:** Traditional systems integrators in the deployment window: what adapting requires, and what it looks like when a firm does not. Written without contempt.

**Eyebrow:** Journal · Essay 02

**Headline:** Some of you will learn to remain.

**Dek:** The conjecture says traditional integrators will modernize and adapt, and that some will not adapt as well. New entrants will be founded in the same window. This essay is for the incumbent who intends to be in the first group.

### Memo to the firm that intends to adapt

You already have what new entrants rent by the week: people who have sat on the floor, who know why the batch window is sacred, who have been blamed for a go-live and stayed anyway. That knowledge is the asset. It does not survive being resold as an "AI factory" slide with a new rate card under the old incentives.

Adapting, concretely, looks like this.

The unit of sale becomes the process and the year of weather, even if your finance system would prefer a tower of hours. The eval gets a decay date, and someone in your firm can refuse an upgrade in writing. The unofficial workbook is on the map. The handback names a keeper inside the customer, and your people are rewarded when that keeper no longer needs them in the room, not only when the hours are full.

You will have to tell some of your best teams that the thing they were excellent at — implementing a known category, and leaving — is still honorable and is no longer the whole engagement. Do not humiliate that skill. Put it on passage and bindings, where it is still the spine, and hire or grow the eval keeper and the loop designer beside it.

If you can do those things, you do not need to become a small firm like the one this website imagines. You may be better than one, because you can already be in fifty buildings on a Monday. Scale is not the enemy. A scale of the old completion, sold into the new work, is the enemy.

### Memo to the firm that will not

You will win work this year. The customer already has a contract, and the contract already has a transformation clause, and the clause can absorb the word agent without absorbing the practice. You will staff it with people who know how to run a program. The program will produce a pane the floor did not ask for. Usage will be reported. The keeper will not exist. Weather will be handled by a ticket.

This will look fine in the steering committee. It will look like clock A on the floor. In a year, the customer will hire a smaller firm to do the process you were already paid for, or they will conclude that agents do not work here. Both outcomes will be explained without reference to the missing decay date. You will move on to the next clause. Nothing in your system will require you to notice.

The conjecture says some will clearly not adapt as well. It does not say they will vanish. It says the window is real. Windows do not close on the unwilling with drama. They close with a series of renewals that feel ordinary.

### To the new entrant, one paragraph

You do not get to sneer. You have no fifty buildings. You have a method and a refusal, and you will be tempted to skip cartography because the customer is eager. Eagerness is not a control. If you cannot decline a process, you are the old pattern with a newer noun.

### Close

If you are an integrator building the first memo, the begin letter is open to you. Say which incentive you are willing to change. That sentence is the qualification.
