## Copy

**Browser title:** The firm — Feld Company

**Meta description:** What Feld is: a forward-deployed firm that installs agents into a single process and remains for the model weather. No headquarters. Pods sit on the customer's floor.

**Eyebrow:** The firm

**Headline:** A firm the size of a pod, hired into one process at a time.

**Dek:** Feld exists because the deployment layer is a practice, not a feature. We are small on purpose. The work does not survive being handed to a bench of strangers every quarter.

### What we are

We are forward-deployed. The cartography, the shadow week, and the hard conversations about release happen in the customer's building, on the floor where the queue already lives. Between visits the pod works wherever it works, and the weather log stays current.

We are model-neutral. The binding is ours to recommend and ours to refuse. A model that retains what it must not, or a model the eval will not carry, does not get the queue. We do not train a foundation model and we do not ask to be admired for that.

We are not a systems integrator with a new slide, and we are not a software vendor hoping services will be a temporary embarrassment. Services are the product. The software we leave behind is the binding, the eval harness, the attestation, and the runbook.

### What a pod is

A lead who can read a process and change a binding. A cartographer or an eval keeper, sometimes the same person in a smaller install. An industry principal on a lighter cadence, who has seen this floor before and will say when this company is not special. Security reviews the binding before any write-access, whether or not anyone finds that romantic.

The customer names a counterpart who can open doors and, later, a keeper who will own the eval when we step aside. Without that keeper, we do not start the last month. The handback is the product.

### Where we will not sit

Inside a licensed profession, practicing it. Inside a decision about a person who cannot see the reason. Inside a process the sponsor cannot name. On a stage, as the transformation. Those refusals are written more carefully on the manifesto and in the begin form, which asks what must never be automated before it asks for a budget.
