## Copy

**Browser title:** Forward-deployed engineer — Feld Company

**Meta description:** A day in the seat that makes the binding and stays for shadow week. What you will have done before we call the work yours.

**Eyebrow:** Roles · Seat 01

**Headline:** Forward-deployed engineer.

**Dek:** You put the agent where the work already happens, and you are still there when the first week of real files disagrees with the design. The title is fashionable. The Tuesday is not.

### The day

**7:50.** On their floor. You have a badge that expires. You sit at the side desk the handler actually uses, not the innovation room two floors up.

**8:30.** The thin binding is read-only and it has already failed on a file with a duplicate claimant. You read the failure with the handler, not in a channel later. You write down the true name of the failure in her words.

**10:15.** You change the binding. You do not widen its permissions because widening would make the morning quieter. Write-access is still closed. Idris has not signed it. You do not route around Idris.

**13:00.** You eat with the team if you are invited and you do not interview them while they eat. Cartography has its own hours.

**15:40.** A sponsor asks if this can cover a second process by Friday. You say what the eval covers, which is this process. You offer the sentence Leila would want: the second process gets a map, or it gets nothing.

**18:10.** Off the floor. You write the weather-relevant note, even though no model changed today: three cases that should enter Ruth's set. Then you stop. The evening is not a silent second shift.

### What you bring

You have built against systems you did not own: a claim platform, an ERP, a permit system, a queue with opinions. You can read an API and you can also read a screen that will never have one, and say so early. You write clearly enough that a keeper can operate the binding after you step aside.

You are not owed a greenfield. If that is the only work you want, this seat will feel like a punishment, and we would rather say that here.

### What the seat is not

It is not a pre-sales costume. It is not a resident who sends every question back to a distant product team. There is no distant product team. The binding is the product, and you are one of the people who changes it.

### Close

If the day sounds like a day you want, say so in the letter, and name a system you had to learn in front of its owners.
