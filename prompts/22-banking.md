## Copy

**Browser title:** Banking — Feld Company

**Meta description:** Operational banking work: KYC refresh and the first draft of a credit memo. Controls stay with the people who already own them.

**Eyebrow:** Industries · Banking

**Headline:** The control that will not move, stays.

**Dek:** We enter a bank through the operational process, not through the trading floor and not through a slogan about the future of finance. Two processes are enough to say whether we understand the floor.

### KYC refresh

The file is already dull, which is why it hurts. A refresh is a pile of documents, a prior decision, a list of what changed, and a person who will sign that the customer is still known. An agent can assemble the pack and list the deltas. The person who signs remains the person who signs. A faster pack that obscures a delta is a worse pack.

The eval here is made of refreshes the floor already closed, including ones that should have been escalated and were. If the agent cannot reproduce the escalation, it does not get the ordinary files either.

### The credit memo's first draft

A memo has a house voice and a credit policy. The agent drafts inside both, from the numbers the system of record actually holds, not from a narrative a relationship manager pasted in hope. The relationship manager remains accountable for the judgment. We will not install a draft that flatters the borrower in a way the policy would not.

### Controls, named

| Control | Who keeps it |
|---|---|
| Identity of the customer | The named KYC officer, deputy on the loop card |
| Decision to refresh off-cycle | The same, never the agent |
| Credit recommendation | The author of the memo |
| Exception to policy | The committee that already owns exceptions |
| What may be used as an example | The person whose writing it is, asked |

If a sponsor asks the agent to "handle KYC," we rewrite the sentence until it fits this sheet, or we decline.

### Close

Bring the control you will not move. We will tell you in the first conversation if the process is really that control, or something quieter beside it.
