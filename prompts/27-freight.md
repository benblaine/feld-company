## Copy

**Browser title:** Freight — Feld Company

**Meta description:** Yard, dwell, and the customer update. Appointments that were never real. Messages that are allowed to be dull and true.

**Eyebrow:** Industries · Freight

**Headline:** Say what the yard is actually doing.

**Dek:** Freight operations fail in the gap between the appointment and the gate. An agent that writes a confident update into that gap will be believed once. We install updates that the yard board can support.

### The board

Trucks, doors, dwell, the load that was supposed to be here. The board is usually a mix of a system and a person's head. Passage and access apply. Until the board is a record the agent can read, the agent does not speak to a customer.

### The update

A customer update has three honest forms. It is here. It is not here, and this is the reason we actually know. We do not know, and the next time we will. The agent may draft only the form the binding supports. "Your freight is on the way" is not a form we use. It is a wish.

The person on the desk releases updates that leave the building. A sample may move to review only after the eval has lived through a peak, not before.

### The appointment that was a fiction

Some appointments exist so a spreadsheet balances. Cartography names them. Redraw either makes them real or stops mentioning them. An agent that politely confirms a fiction is an expensive way to lie.

### Close

Show us the update you are least proud of. The install starts from that sentence, rewritten until it is true.

**Stamp:** Begin from the yard
**Text link:** Retail operations, a different kind of schedule
