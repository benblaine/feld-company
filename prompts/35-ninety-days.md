## Copy

**Browser title:** First ninety days — Feld Company

**Meta description:** A week-by-week shape for the start of an install: cartography, access, a thin binding, shadow week, and a narrow release. Ranges, not a promise.

**Eyebrow:** Practice · Ninety days

**Headline:** A calendar with room for the floor to correct us.

**Dek:** Ninety days is a shape. A freeze, a vendor, or a process that was misnamed can stretch it. We would rather show the shape and keep faith when it changes than publish a countdown.

### Weeks 1 and 2 · Cartography

The map, including the unofficial path and the exception-keeper. The freeze calendar. The never-list, first draft. No binding yet, except a read of something already permitted, if it helps the map.

### Weeks 3 and 4 · Access and the thin binding

The four names. A read-only binding to the system of use or the system of record, on their tenant, signed in. The first failure, encountered on their floor, written in their words.

### Weeks 5 and 6 · Redraw and the loop card

The one-page redraw, corrected by the people who run the path. The loop card with names. The eval's first twelve files, chosen with the keeper, even if the keeper is still only tentative.

### Weeks 7 and 8 · Shadow week, then a narrow release

The agent drafts beside the human. Disagreements are recorded. At the end, either a release right on a slice so narrow the floor suggested it, or a decision to wait. Both are successful weeks.

### Weeks 9 to 13 · The cadence begins

The decay date arrives once. The weather log has at least one line, even if the line is "no material change, set reviewed." The handback note is started, not finished. We are still in the building more than we will be in month six.

### Close

If you need it faster, the honest answer is a narrower process, not a skipped week.
