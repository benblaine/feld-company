## Copy

**Browser title:** Journal — Feld Company

**Meta description:** Longer writing from the firm: an essay on stepping aside, and an essay on the integrator's window. Few pieces, kept current, no content mill.

**Eyebrow:** Journal

**Headline:** Two essays, until there is a reason for a third.

**Dek:** We will not publish in order to look alive. These two are the ones the rest of the site keeps pointing at. When a third exists, it will be because a labor changed.

### Contents

**Stepping aside.** What it means to leave a floor without vanishing and without staying forever. The handback, said in longer sentences.
`/journal/stepping-aside`

**The integrator's window.** Traditional firms will adapt unevenly. A note to the ones who intend to, and a clear view of the ones who will not, written without contempt.
`/journal/the-integrator`

### A note on comments and subscriptions

There is no comment form and no newsletter. If you want to argue with an essay, the letter at the end of the site is a better room than a thread. A person reads it.

### Close

The letters to the three seats are not essays. They are shorter, and they are next if you are deciding whether to write.

**Stamp:** Read the letters
**Text link:** Or begin

## Layout

Posture: **Contents.**

A contents page of a journal, not a blog grid. Two entries, each a full-width row with a large display title, a one-sentence dek (the sentences in the contents section), and a mono index "Essay 01" and "Essay 02". Hairline between. The whole row is the link.

Plenty of empty page below. The emptiness is the point. Do not fill it with "related reading" cards.

A small mono line under the headline: "Issued as needed. Not a feed."

Stamp `/letters`. Text link `/begin`.
